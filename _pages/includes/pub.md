Energy-efficient Probabilistic Ising Machine with Voltage-controlled MRAM and Reconfigurable Hamiltonian Computing Unit
\\
Y. Hou*, <strong>Y. Lu</strong>*, et al. *(In preparation)*

An energy-efficient probabilistic Ising machine using VCMA-MTJ devices for spin generation and a reconfigurable Hamiltonian computing unit for mixed-precision optimization workloads.

The proposed 28 nm VCMA-MTJ P-bit chip provides 96 Kb capacity, 8-bit I/O, 5 ns read time, and 148 fJ/bit read energy.

BitStack: A Same-Bit Stacking Architecture for Adaptive-Bitwidth Inference \\
C. Zhou*, <strong>Y. Lu</strong>*, et al. *(Co-author; submitted)*

An accelerator architecture for input-dependent mixed-precision inference that combines bit-stacking computation, early stopping, and hardware-software co-design.
