Energy-efficient Probabilistic Ising Machine with Voltage-controlled MRAM and Reconfigurable Hamiltonian Computing Unit \\
Y. Hou*, <strong>Y. Lu</strong>* et al. *(Co-author; in preparation)*

An energy-efficient probabilistic Ising machine that uses VCMA-MTJ devices for probabilistic spin generation and a reconfigurable Hamiltonian computing unit for mixed-precision optimization workloads.

The associated 28 nm VCMA-MTJ P-bit chip provides 96 Kb capacity, 8-bit I/O, 5 ns read time, and 148 fJ/bit read energy.
