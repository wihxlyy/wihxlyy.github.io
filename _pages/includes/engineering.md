# Engineering Skills

## Digital and Accelerator Design

- Performance evaluation of existing accelerators through profiling with `perf` and C/C++ modeling of compute, memory access, and energy behavior.
- RTL design of specialized functional units for SoC-based accelerators in Verilog, integrated with a RISC-V core, shared memory, and DMA.
- FPGA-level prototyping and board demonstrations of designed accelerators (Vivado, ZYNQ-7020, Basys3).
- Logic synthesis and power/latency/area evaluation across PVT corners with Synopsys Design Compiler.

## Hardware Verification

- Functional verification of digital blocks with UVM and SystemVerilog.
- Formal property verification of digital blocks with Cadence JasperGold.
- Waveform-based debug with Synopsys VCS/Verdi, and Verilog lint checking with nLint.

## VLSI and Device Design

- Analog in-memory-computing circuit design based on emerging non-volatile memory devices, including VCMA-MTJ and dual-mode spintronic memristors.
- Compact modeling of emerging devices, including LLG-based VCMA-MTJ switching dynamics in Python.
- Full-custom design flow in Cadence Virtuoso, covering schematic, layout, DRC/LVS, and post-layout simulation.
