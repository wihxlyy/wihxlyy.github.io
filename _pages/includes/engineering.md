# Engineering Skills

## Digital and Simulator Design

- Performance evaluation of existing accelerators through C-based simulation, measuring computation, memory traffic, speedup, and energy against published baselines.
- RTL design of specialized functional units for SoC-based accelerators in Verilog, together with the surrounding compute and control logic.
- FPGA-level demonstration of designed accelerators, from synthesis and waveform bring-up to on-board functional validation.

## Hardware Verification

- Functional verification of digital blocks with UVM and SystemVerilog, including testbench construction and coverage-driven simulation.
- Functional verification of digital blocks with JasperGold, using assertion- and property-based checks.

## VLSI and Device Design

- Analog in-memory-computing circuit design built on emerging non-volatile memory devices.
- Compact modeling of emerging devices, capturing switching dynamics for circuit-level simulation.
